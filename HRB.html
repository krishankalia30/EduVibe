To My Beautiful Sisters ❤️
✦ Memories • Love • Forever ✦

There are so many things in life that change with time, but one thing I know will always remain the same — the bond we share.

We may argue over the smallest things, annoy each other for absolutely no reason, and sometimes wonder how we even survive each other's craziness 😂... But behind all those little fights is something much bigger — a love, a friendship, and a connection that I will always treasure.

Thank you for being there through the good days, the difficult days, the random laughs, the unforgettable memories, and all those little moments that somehow became the stories we will remember forever.

A Rakhi is just a thread... but the promise behind it is so much more. It represents protection, trust, understanding, memories, and a bond that distance or time can never erase. 🫶

No matter where life takes us, no matter how much we grow, and no matter how busy life becomes... you will always have a special place in my heart.

Here's to all the childhood memories, all the crazy moments still waiting to happen, all the laughter yet to come, and a lifetime of being there for each other.

With lots of love,
Your Brother ❤️ Krishan Kalia
